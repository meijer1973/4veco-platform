## 4. Concepts And Terminology

| Concept | Meaning | Formula and condition | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constante kosten | Total does not change with Q within period/capacity | TCK is sum of fixed period amounts | Classifying by invoice name or frequency |
| Variabele kosten | Total changes with Q | TVK=vQ only when v is constant per product | Saying variable always means changing per product |
| Totale kosten | Entire production in one period | TK=TCK+TVK | Dropping the time-period unit |
| Gemiddelde kosten | Relevant total divided over products | GCK=TCK/Q; GVK=TVK/Q; GTK=TK/Q=GCK+GVK, Q>0 | Treating TCK as fixed cost per product |
