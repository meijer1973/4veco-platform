## Goal

Bring v6 to its planning end state: a stable 11-book, three-year
exam-training contract that can govern target-exercise production without
requiring further structural curriculum design.

This is not the student-facing product end state. The product end state still
requires paragraph-level route proof from readiness to target-equivalent proof,
with prompt, source, correction-model, operation, answer-form, practice, and
exit-ticket evidence.

## Scope

This bundle combines:

- `Y3-EXAM-ANCHOR-INGESTION-WAVE-1`
- `THREE-YEAR-BLUEPRINT-MATURITY-REVIEW-1`

It is report-only planning authority. It does not mutate protected references,
create target-registry rows, mint MTUs, create lessons, close operations,
authorize product routes, close CP-6 or Scale Gate, or authorize diagnostics,
mastery, PV, summative use, or student/product use.
