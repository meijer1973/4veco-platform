## Goal

## Quality Standard

The implementation must satisfy the active specification, preserve the Book 1 and Chapter 2.1 student-facing quality floor, and produce rendered output that a reviewer can inspect at normal reading scale. Student-facing theory, exercises, examples, tables, graphs, figure assets, and answer models must trace to the 2.2.1 target intent and to the readiness brief.

Closure proof must include rendered-page proof, figure proof, validator/test evidence, lead review, command-log evidence, and classified follow-up records for any unresolved target, notation, graph/table, output, or stale-bundle issue. PASS WITH FLAGS may carry only non-core future work and may not carry missing rendered proof, missing answer models, visible student-facing defects, unreadable figures, graph/text contradictions, stale generated output, or reviewed-final target claims.
