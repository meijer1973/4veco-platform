## Gap this sprint addresses

The current canonical product baseline is mostly operational and
student-route oriented. The repositories do not yet fully encode a strategic
product vision that explains:

- where 4veco intends to build a moat;
- where competitive parity is required even without distinct advantage;
- how the MTU tree and exercise-first architecture improve learning
  efficiency;
- how lean open diffusion, no accounts, local-only progress state, and
  static-first design shape product decisions;
- how motivation, affordance, and five-minute-understandable micro teaching
  units should guide trade-offs;
- how repository design, validators, maps, review agents, and evidence logs
  are part of the product because they let agents work longer and safer.

This sprint adds that strategic layer without changing generated lesson
output or authorizing scale/product claims.
