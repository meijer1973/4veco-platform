## Didactic strengths, risks, transfer and retention

The strongest feature is the deliberate connection between totals in a table, line positions, algebraic equality and economic meaning. The TO line is introduced before TK, then the intersection and profit distance are added. Captions and text explain why the cost intercept is positive and why profit is not an area. Solid/dashed curves and direct labels reinforce colour differences. The actual full pages keep the relevant graph reasoning adjacent to its diagram.

The mandatory rounding check means a student who skips the optional guided route still encounters noninteger interpretation before the bakery target. The worked/independent numerical break-even examples are whole numbers, so the bakery is the first full noninteger calculation on the short route; this is a modest transfer step supported by explicit theory and 2c, not an uncovered operation. If later authorised classroom observations show difficulty here, reconsider the independent values without adding workload. No such observation is claimed now.

Transfer covers calculation, graphs, numerical tables, written explanations, familiar enterprise contexts and a target-style question. The bonus changes the fixed-price assumption and asks students to choose evidence, offering cognitive flexibility. A news article, simulation or game is absent and is not needed to complete this paper target. Retention is supported by actual retrieval of earlier cost relations in 1 and 9; later §2.1.3/2.1.4 reuse is a planned course connection, not proof that durable retention has occurred.
