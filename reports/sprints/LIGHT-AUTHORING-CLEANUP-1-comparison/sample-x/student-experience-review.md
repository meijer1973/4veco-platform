## What the student will understand

- Revenue is the amount received from sales; costs must still be subtracted before it becomes profit.
- A fixed unit price makes GO equal to P only when Q is positive.
- Break-even is an equality of total revenue and total costs, represented by their intersection.
- A fractional model quantity and the first feasible whole number without loss answer different questions. The target answer verifies 714 and 715 explicitly.
- Profit at one quantity is the difference between two euro amounts on the same vertical line. The loss/profit zones name quantities; their areas are not profit amounts.

## Where the student may get lost and motivation risks

No major confusion risk remains. Two residual effort points are worth improving, but existing instruction is sufficient for the intended typical student:

1. **Algebra operation visibility.** The prose on P4 tells the student to subtract the variable-cost term and divide. The worked equations on P5/E1 show the resulting lines without side annotations. A mathematically less confident reader must connect that prose across a page turn. Add a short annotation beside each worked transition, such as “aan beide kanten −1,20Q” and “deel beide kanten door 1,80”. This reinforces the existing method without adding a new operation.
2. **Optional graph-base precision.** The badge base on P8/E4 has widely spaced ticks and no grid. Students can interpolate, but locating (25;100) and the vertical endpoints requires some ruler work. Light gridlines or a few small, unlabeled intermediate ticks would make this support exercise easier to use while still leaving TO and all answer markings to the student.

The repeated four-step method is useful consolidation, while varied contexts and the two-price bonus provide sufficient change. The plan estimates 51 minutes plus 4 minutes for feedback for the core. That is a planning estimate, not measured learner performance. The complete optional support route takes 68 minutes including feedback; this must remain a deliberately scheduled longer route. The printed compact route and neutral skip wording prevent the support section from appearing compulsory for everyone. Sparse final pages add paper use but do not create a learning blocker.
