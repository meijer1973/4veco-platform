## Changes

- Updated `build-scripts/platform/build-landing-page.js` so paragraph landing
  pages render route layers:
  - `Start`
  - `Leer`
  - `Oefen`
  - optional `Check`
  - `Verdiep`
- `Check` is hidden unless an exit-ticket/checkpoint surface exists.
- `Oefen` now presents guided support plus scoped practice routes.
- `Rekenen` routes to the generated `stappenplan` surface when it exists.
- The full `wiskundevaardigheden` skill map is demoted to a collapsed advanced
  source item when a scoped calculation/procedure route exists.
- Summary, news, presentation, textbook, and source/download surfaces are
  demoted to collapsed `Verdiep` groups.
- Student-facing Word exercise rows remain hidden from paragraph landings.
- `scripts/qa-student-web-pages.js` now validates paragraph route layers
  instead of assuming every resource-grid page must expose a graph route.
- `scripts/tests/build-landing-page.test.js` covers the new route IA and keeps
  legacy Word rows out of generated student landing pages.
