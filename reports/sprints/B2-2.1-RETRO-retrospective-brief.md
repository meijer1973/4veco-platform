# Sprint B2-2.1-RETRO: Retrospective Brief

## Verdict

Proceed to `B2-2.2-A` only after this sprint closes with lead review, because the
human-found assembled-exercise defect is now repaired in both validation and
Book 2 Chapter 2.1 output. No separate hardening sprint is needed before 2.2 for
the exercise-assembly issue, but the next chapter sprint must keep the repaired
chapter validator in its acceptance tests.

## What worked

- The paragraph-level production workflow created visible theory, exercise,
  answer, asset, review, and quality-ref output for 2.1.1 through 2.1.4.
- Chapter and book health checks already covered folder structure, paragraph
  artifacts, asset references, and review-file presence.
- Lead review on `B2-2.1-A` correctly preserved the non-blocking carry flags
  instead of pretending the vertical slice was final source proof.

## What failed

The assembled chapter did not include the exercise files for the theory
paragraphs. The exercises existed in the paragraph folders, but the student
chapter/book assembly omitted them. The old `validate-chapter.js` still passed,
which means `B2-2.1-A` overclaimed validation coverage.

The defect is a process blocker unless repaired: a textbook chapter without its
paragraph exercises is not acceptable printed textbook output.

## Corrective Repair

- `scripts/validate-chapter.js` now checks every theory paragraph section in the
  assembled `hoofdstuk.md` and fails if the corresponding `opgaven.md` content
  is absent or incomplete.
- The stale Book 2 Chapter 2.1 build failed the repaired validator with missing
  exercises for 2.1.1 and 2.1.2 and incomplete exercises for 2.1.3.
- `build_chapter.py` now appends each theory paragraph's `opgaven.md` under
  `## Opgaven` in the main chapter assembly.
- The chapter CSS now follows Book 1's chapter print rhythm more closely:
  `@page` margin `2cm 2.2cm 2.2cm 3.2cm`, body `font-size: 11pt`, and
  `line-height: 1.45`.
- Exercise HTML wrapping now handles heading-style `## Opgave` blocks as well
  as bold-paragraph exercise markers.

## Repeatability Decision

The workflow is repeatable enough to open `B2-2.2-A` after lead review, but only
with the amended validation standard. The important lesson is that paragraph
validity and chapter/book validity are not the same thing: every future chapter
sprint must prove that paragraph exercises are present in the assembled student
chapter, not merely present somewhere in the folder tree.

## Recommendation

Close `B2-2.1-RETRO` as `PASS WITH FLAGS` if lead review agrees. Then open
`B2-2.2-A` with an explicit acceptance test for assembled exercises, Book 1 print
layout parity, chapter validation, book validation, and lead review before
closure.
