# Sprint B2-2.1.1-HARDEN: Quality Notes

## What changed

2.1.1 now follows the Book 1 paragraph shape:

- motivating problem before notation;
- theory in small conceptual blocks;
- definition and formula boxes;
- worked example with a repeatable procedure;
- summary and help note;
- staged exercises from graph reading to target exercise and challenge item;
- answer model with substitutions, units, and economic conclusions.

## Learning from this paragraph

The biggest quality gain came from adding the exercise route, not from adding
more theory. The original paragraph already had the correct formulas, but it
did not give students enough practice time or enough varied representations.

For 2.1.2, keep the sprint small and reuse this pattern:

1. Start with a concrete business problem.
2. Teach one formula cluster at a time.
3. Put a worked example before exercises.
4. Make the target exercise the final normal exercise, not the only serious exercise.
5. Record whether a new graph is needed before writing the answer model.
