## Summary

Integrated the root golden package for `2.1.3 Marginale kosten en marginale
opbrengsten` into the canonical Book 2 paragraph folder. The paragraph now has
a Book 1-style route for marginal values: motivating problem, doeloefening,
table-difference explanation, two visual supports, worked examples, staged
exercises, target-equivalent table production, and answer-model support.

The carried `MO = MK` decision was resolved narrowly. Students learn that
`MO > MK` raises profit for one extra step, `MO < MK` lowers profit for one
extra step, and `MO = MK` leaves profit unchanged for that step. The paragraph
does not teach the full profit-maximisation rule or calculus.

Rebuilt the 2.1.3 paragraph, exercise, and answer outputs, then rebuilt Chapter
2.1 as a combined chapter and answer packet. Lead review round 1 and round 2
closed `PASS WITH FLAGS`; the sprint is marked completed after final
complete-bundle validation.
