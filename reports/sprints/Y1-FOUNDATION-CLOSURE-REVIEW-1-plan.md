## Purpose

authored-registry and evidence-planning level after the follow-up blocker
lanes merged through `B1-COLLECTIVE-DEMAND-KINK-DISPOSITION-1` and the later

Scale Gate, diagnostics, mastery, PV, or student/product use can close. It asks
whether the Book 1 target-registry foundation and its evidence plan are clean
enough to stop carrying the old placeholder, migrated-record, graph/table,
normal/inferior-good, simultaneous-shift, and collective-demand dropout
blockers as Year 1 foundation-planning blockers.
