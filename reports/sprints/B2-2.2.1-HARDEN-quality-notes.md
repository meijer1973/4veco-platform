- `P`, `Qv`, and `Ev` notation;
- `%dP` and `%dQv` using old values;
- `Ev = %dQv / %dP`;
- signed `Ev` interpretation;
- `|Ev|` classification;
- economic explanation based on alternatives, necessity, luxury, budget share, and time;
- a five-part answer structure for context-heavy tasks.

The exercises include start tasks, independent practice, index/percentage interleaving, the cinema/petrol target exercise, a table exercise, and an answer-evaluation exercise.
