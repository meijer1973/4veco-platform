## Result

`THREE-YEAR-BLUEPRINT-MATURITY-BUNDLE-1` consolidates v6 into a stable
planning baseline:

- The 4+4+3 route is fixed: Books 1-4, Books 5-8, and Books 9-11.
- All eleven books have a stable role, test-week placement, retrieval function,
  and bridge to the next book.
- The working load model is resolved:
  - Book 5 has 13 count-bearing candidates; `Y2-B5-P06` is absorbed into the
    Book 5/6 bridge and is not standalone count-bearing.
  - Book 6 has 13 count-bearing candidates; `Y2-B6-P13` remains deferred and
    non-count-bearing.
  - Book 11 has 9 count-bearing modules plus 4 timed-paper/repair cycles.
- Every candidate status is known through the final status index and source
  row-level matrices.
- The Year 3 evidence overlay records prompt/source/correction-model/point,
  operation, answer-form, target, MTU, and blocker status for the priority
  macro families.
- The production critical path starts with evidence-backed target packets,
  then MTU/task-family review, then paragraph-production lanes.
- Change control freezes v6 as the planning baseline after human approval.
