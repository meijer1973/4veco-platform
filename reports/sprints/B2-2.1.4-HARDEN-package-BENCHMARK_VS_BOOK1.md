# Benchmark versus Book 1 - 2.1.4

## Verdict

`2.1.4 Gemengde opgaven: kosten en opbrengsten` is a golden-candidate mixed-practice paragraph. It exceeds the Book 1 baseline on source-first integration and agent-copyability, while preserving the Book 1 strengths of doeloefening, worked example, visual support, and answer-model clarity.

## Concrete improvements over a normal theory paragraph

- It does not pretend that a mixed paragraph needs new theory.
- It makes the student choose between earlier procedures instead of being told which formula to use.
- It uses two-source target design: normal production formulas first, marginal table reasoning second.
- It makes graph interpretation operational: break-even means `TO = TK`; profit at one quantity is a vertical distance.
- It preserves the `2.1.3` warning that marginal values are between rows and does not introduce differentiation or the later profit-maximisation rule.

## Golden-example criterion

A future mixed paragraph can copy this structure:

1. State no new theory.
2. Show the integration route.
3. Provide a formula card from prior paragraphs.
4. Work one integrated example.
5. Give exercises that force source selection and procedure choice.
6. Answer with calculations plus economic interpretation.
7. Keep a governance note if the integration target is locally proposed.
