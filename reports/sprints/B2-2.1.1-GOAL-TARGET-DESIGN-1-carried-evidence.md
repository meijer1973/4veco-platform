## Accepted four-goal seed

1. Classify cost components from behaviour within a stated period and relevant
   production range, including one expense category with both constant and
   variable components.
2. Construct and use `TCK`, `TVK`, and `TK` functions from a context.
3. Calculate and interpret `GCK`, `GVK`, and `GTK` with correct units.
4. Explain and compare total-versus-average cost movements under a stated
   constant-variable-cost-per-unit assumption.

## Accepted target seed

The seed uses Bakkerij De Korenaar within 500–1,000 loaves per month with the
same premises and ovens: €440/month rent and insurance; €60/month connection
and subscription; €0.70/loaf flour and packaging; and €0.10 extra oven
electricity per additional loaf. It asks for behaviour-based classification,
function construction, a complete first calculation set at `Q=500`, a reduced
comparison set at `Q=1000`, correct euro/month and euro/loaf dimensions, and
one integrated evaluation of the claim that all totals double and all averages
halve. Its 13.5-minute estimate is provisional until a fresh specialist
walkthrough is recorded.
