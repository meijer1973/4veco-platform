# Sprint B2-2.1-A: Book 2 Chapter 2.1 Part A Vertical Slice

## Goal

Build the first Book 2 printed textbook vertical slice for Chapter 2.1: 2.1.1 Kostenstructuren, 2.1.2 Opbrengsten, winst en break-even, 2.1.3 Marginale kosten en marginale opbrengsten, and 2.1.4 Gemengde opgaven: kosten en opbrengsten. The sprint must produce student-facing printed output and answer material through the normal lesson workflow, while resolving the target-readiness flags carried from `B2-READY-1`.

## Context

`B2-READY-1` closed PASS WITH FLAGS and authorized proceeding to this sprint. Its carried flags are entry conditions here:

- `2.1.4` remains a placeholder target and must be replaced or explicitly resolved before Chapter 2.1 can be considered production-ready.
- `2.1.1` through `2.1.3` are migrated targets needing v5 review, so they are usable as starting targets, not final proof.
- The textbook end-state description is an initial draft, not a locked specification.

No Book 2 output folder exists yet in `../4veco-lessen`, so this sprint creates the first Book 2 printed-output structure.

## Quality Standard

The implementation must satisfy the active specification, maintain the quality floor established by Book 1 print work, and produce rendered output that a reviewer can inspect. Student-facing text, exercises, graphs, tables, and answer models must trace to target-exercise intent or explicitly documented target-gap decisions. Closure proof must include paragraph/chapter validators, generated PDFs or equivalent rendered output, lead review, and named follow-up records for any unresolved target, graph, notation, or style issue.

## Operationalized sprint procedure

1. Start from the `B2-READY-1` carried flags. Stop if the sprint cannot record a concrete 2.1.4 target-gap decision before writing final mixed-practice exercises.
2. Create or confirm the Book 2 folder and Chapter 2.1 folder structure in `../4veco-lessen/`.
3. Draft the 2.1.4 mixed-practice target design or explicitly record why the gap is carried. Confirm whether 2.1.1 through 2.1.3 are accepted as v5 production targets for this slice or require local revisions.
4. Build 2.1.1, 2.1.2, and 2.1.3 as Part A paragraphs with theory, examples, exercises, answers, and assets. Build 2.1.4 as a gemengde-opgaven section with exercises and answers only unless a later decision changes the contract.
5. Generate rendered paragraph/chapter output through the lesson workflow. Validate paragraph, chapter, asset, and print checks. Stop for correction if rendered output, graph/table assets, notation, or answer models fail the quality floor.
6. Prepare result, diff summary, command log, and lead review files. Closure requires lead review and complete bundle validation.
