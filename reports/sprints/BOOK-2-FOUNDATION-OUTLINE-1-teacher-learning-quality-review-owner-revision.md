   profit, break-even, marginal cost, `MO=MK`, and formal

   §§2.1.2–2.1.4 and the §2.3.2 supply-as-MC bridge.
4. **Relevant-range teaching — PASS.** The plan must state time period,
   production/capacity range, capacity changes, and the bounded rather than
   universal status of linear `TVK`/constant `GVK`.
5. **Planning ownership — PASS.** Part A owns `X.Y.Z-textbook-plan.md` and the
   complete foundation decision. Part B owns `_paragraph-plan.md`, consumes the
   approved Part A plan/handoff, and is instructed to return foundation changes
   to Part A.
6. **Action-specific readiness — PASS.** §2.1.1 goal design, target design, and
   specialist review are permitted while approval and production remain
   blocked. This supports useful pedagogical design without presenting pending
