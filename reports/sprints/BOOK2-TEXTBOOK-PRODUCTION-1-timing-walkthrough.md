## Observation and correction protocol

During first actual use, record component times, source-reading/plotting/writing
load, student questions, incomplete operations, errors and support needs. Compare
the unassisted and supported routes without diagnosing mastery from placement
or a single correct answer. Allocate the printed extra-support time explicitly
and retain access to normal teacher checking and the separate answers.

If a core does not fit, re-plan instruction/repetition and scheduling with
independent review. Do not delete frozen target parts, mandatory concepts,
independent practice or required graph production, and do not shrink 12 pt text
to manufacture feasibility. Substantive target/authority changes require their
own owner decision; this project cannot normalize them away.

Final package review must challenge every complete route and retain the
observation flag unless actual classroom evidence is supplied. No measured
attainment or inspection-framework compliance is asserted by this inventory.
