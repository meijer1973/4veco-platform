## Goal

Turn the four package reasoning-game prototypes into durable repository
capability:

- versioned golden-exemplar library and manifest;
- cross-exemplar product and transfer standard;
- stable policy with change notes;
- dedicated `econ-reasoning-game` skill;
- shared task-family promotion without another mode-overloaded engine;
- validators and negative fixtures for recurring defects;
- rendered fidelity gallery for all four exemplars;
- blind transfer to one unseen paragraph;
- specialist and two-round lead review;
- one final human gate, `GATE-REASONING-GOLDEN-FAMILY-1`.

Core rule:

```text
copy product grammar
re-derive reasoning grammar
```

## Authority Boundary

This goal creates golden-reference memory and reusable authoring capability.
It does not authorize student rollout, target-equivalent proof, diagnostics,
mastery or sequencing decisions, summative use, PV projection, Scale Gate
claims, or broad replacement of existing reasoning games.

Generated lesson output must not be hand-edited.

## Review Standard

Reviewers must judge rendered student experience and interaction states, not
only file existence. A passing review explains:

- why each composition copies product grammar but re-derives reasoning grammar;
- why the blind transfer is not a clone of an exemplar mechanic;
- why the final answer is visibly constructed from reasoning functions;
- why graph evidence is interacted with directly rather than used decoratively;
- why negative fixtures catch known recurring defects;
- why authority boundaries remain false.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before final gate if:

- generated lesson output changes by hand;
- a composition exposes a mode picker or internal metadata;
- the goal gives away the answer chain;
- graph evidence is replaced by graph construction or a decorative image;
- final answer construction degrades to a generic textarea only;
- rendered proof lacks partial, retry, correct, mobile, or dark-mode states;
- a reviewer returns an unresolved blocking finding.
