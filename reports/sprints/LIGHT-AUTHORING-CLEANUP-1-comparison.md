## Common production-closure blocker

`skills/econ-quality-control.md` sections 0.3 and 0.5 mandate a reference update
when the freshness check finds changes. The reference was last verified on
2026-04-12; the Dutch Inspectorate has published August 2026 changes:
https://www.onderwijsinspectie.nl/onderwerpen/onderzoekskader-2021-wat-is-er-veranderd/bijstelling-2026.
The owner explicitly excludes standards/reference updates from this cleanup.
The unchanged skill contains no isolated-trial exemption. Therefore neither arm
may claim a completed quality-control record, a full production PASS or
student-use approval. An explicitly INCOMPLETE/BLOCKED inventory record may
preserve the content verdict and the outstanding freshness dependency; it is
not successful completion of the quality-control stage.

Authoring, rendering, independent content inspection and their defects/repairs
can be compared within that boundary. Required quality-reference closure and
full production validation remain outstanding. Any apparent time difference is
also confounded by local setup and shared reviewer-slot waiting, and one
paragraph cannot establish general review effectiveness.
The trial changes A and B together and includes normal authoring variability;
it cannot separate their individual effects. The A-only and B-only runs were
rollback/source checks, not additional paragraph experiments.

Asset-flag counts also need interpretation. The original A5 wording retains both
SVG and PNG files while prohibiting files not referenced in Markdown. Sample X
records its retained SVG sources as a literal unreferenced-file flag; Sample Y's
audit explicitly checks referenced **basenames/pairs**, with no orphaned basename.
These are different audit criteria, so Y's zero reported flags does not establish
a cleaner literal A5 result. Both preserve their editable SVG sources. This
cleanup leaves that existing wording unchanged; no full A5/production closure or
quality gain is inferred from the different flag treatment.
