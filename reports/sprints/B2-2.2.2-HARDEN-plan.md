# Sprint B2-2.2.2-HARDEN: Book 2 Paragraph 2.2.2 Elasticiteit en omzet Quality Hardening

## Goal

Integrate and harden only Book 2 paragraph 2.2.2 Elasticiteit en omzet as student-facing textbook output with canonical paragraph source, opgaven, antwoorden, rendered output, rendered-page proof, validators, lead-review evidence, and a draft PR pair.

The sprint must use `reports/sprints/B2-2.2-READY-1-readiness-brief.md` as controlling input, build directly on the merged `2.2.1 Prijselasticiteit` output, and keep the route tied to `TO = P x Q`, before/after omzet, `Ev`, elastic/inelastic demand, and bounded pricing advice. It must not introduce profit, cost, marginal reasoning, welfare, target promotion, or later paragraph scope.

## Context

`B2-2.2.1-HARDEN` closed only paragraph 2.2.1, not Chapter 2.2 as a whole. Human instruction on 2026-06-18 opened the next controlled step, `B2-2.2.2-HARDEN`, after the platform and lesson PR pair for 2.2.1 merged.

The active target registry keeps 2.2.2 at `migrated_from_v4_needs_v5_review`. That target asks students to reuse the cinema and petrol cases, calculate `TO = P x Q` before and after a price change, connect the outcome to `Ev`, and state the omzet rule. The readiness brief adds a hard boundary: advice must stay tied to omzet, not profit.

The current lesson Chapter 2.2 output contains only 2.2.1 plus aggregate files. This sprint may add a new 2.2.2 paragraph folder and rebuild the chapter aggregate so it contains 2.2.1 plus 2.2.2 only.

## Quality Standard

The implementation must satisfy the active specification, preserve the Book 1 and Chapter 2.1 student-facing quality floor, and produce rendered output that a reviewer can inspect at normal reading scale. Student-facing theory, exercises, tables, decision rules, and answer models must trace to the 2.2.2 target intent and to the readiness brief.

Closure proof must include rendered-page proof, figure proof or explicit no-figure rationale, validator/test evidence, command-log evidence, result and diff-summary metadata, and REV-STD-1 lead review. PASS WITH FLAGS may carry only non-core future work and may not carry missing rendered proof, missing answer models, visible student-facing defects, table overflow, stale generated output, unreadable figures, or reviewed-final target claims.

Any follow-up must be classified with `blocks`, `does_not_block`, and `proof_required_to_close` so a missing core requirement cannot be carried as PASS WITH FLAGS.
