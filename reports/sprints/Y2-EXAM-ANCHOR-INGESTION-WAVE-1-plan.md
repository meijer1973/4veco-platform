## Purpose

Give every Year 2 book at least one reviewed official exam-anchor family and
test whether the Year 2 root map is supported by CvTE prompts, sources,
correction models, and answer forms. Book 8 uses two families because strategic
interaction and the growth/public-finance bridge are materially different.

This is an ingestion/evidence wave. It is not target creation, MTU minting,
protected-reference mutation, official operation closure, paragraph production,
or product/Scale authority.
