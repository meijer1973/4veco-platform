# Sprint B2-2.1-RETRO: Book 2 Section 2.1 Retrospective

## Goal

Decide whether the Book 2 Chapter 2.1 production workflow is repeatable enough
to continue to Chapter 2.2, and name any hardening work that must happen before
the next textbook-production sprint.

## Context

`B2-2.1-A` closed PASS WITH FLAGS after lead review. It built the first Book 2
Part A printed textbook vertical slice for 2.1.1 through 2.1.4 and created
paragraph/chapter rendered output in `../4veco-lessen`.

The carried flags from `B2-2.1-A` are the entry conditions for this
retrospective:

- 2.1.1 through 2.1.3 remain migrated v4 targets needing final v5 review.
- 2.1.4 is local sprint production evidence, not source-registry proof.
- Pagination is serviceable but loose in places.
- 2.1.3 omits explicit `MO = MK` equality-case treatment.
- The chapter PDF size warning should be watched during later assembly.

After the sprint opened, human review found that the assembled chapter PDF did
not include the theory-paragraph exercise files even though the paragraph
outputs did. That must be treated as a validation defect and a generated-output
repair inside this sprint before any retrospective recommendation may proceed.

## Quality Standard

The retrospective must be evidence-led and must measure the completed vertical
slice against the active textbook specification, student-facing quality floor,
rendered output evidence, and closure proof. It may recommend continuing to 2.2,
pausing for hardening, or opening a focused target/source sprint, but it must
not silently waive carried flags. Every recommendation needs an owner, a next
action, a follow-up disposition, and a blocking/non-blocking disposition.

## Operationalized sprint procedure

1. Record baseline from `B2-2.1-A` closure and carried flags.
2. Inspect the result, diff summary, lead review, lesson-output log, and command
   log from `B2-2.1-A`.
3. Write a retrospective brief that states what worked, what did not, what is
   repeatable, and what needs named follow-up.
4. Repair the chapter validator and Book 2 Chapter 2.1 assembly so stale output
   fails for missing exercises and regenerated output contains the exercises.
5. Decide whether `B2-2.2-A` may open, whether a hardening sprint must come
   first, or whether a human decision is required.
6. Prepare result, diff summary, command log, and lead review files.
7. Run complete-bundle validation before marking the sprint completed.

## Proof Required to Close

Closure proof to close must include a retrospective brief, explicit disposition
of every carried flag from `B2-2.1-A`, proof that assembled-exercise validation
failed the stale build and passed the repaired build, a proceed/pause
recommendation for Chapter 2.2, green sprint validators, test evidence, and
lead-review evidence.

## Rollback plan

Remove the `B2-2.1-RETRO-*` sprint artifacts and restore the textbook sprint
ledger/roadmap row to the previous state with `B2-2.1-A` closed and
`B2-2.1-RETRO` planned. Restore `scripts/validate-chapter.js` and rerun
`build_chapter.py` from the previous accepted state if the corrective assembly
change must be rolled back. No protected reference data should need rollback
because this sprint does not edit that surface.

## Human review required

No human review is required to start this retrospective. Human review is needed
only if the retrospective recommends source-registry mutation, skipping final v5
target review, or changing the textbook sprint sequence beyond the roadmap.
Lead review is required before closure.
