  graph operations may be classified from evidence. Book 1 cost/profit
  exposure is `preview_or_familiarity_only`; formal cost structures and
  averages are `new_formal_learning`.

  and formal proportional/degressive/progressive cost classification.
- Prepares for §§2.1.2, 2.1.3, 2.1.4 and the supply-as-marginal-cost bridge in

  bounded assumptions, not universal laws.
