## Mixed Case Progression

1. Book 9 starts with fiscal cases that retrieve Book 8 public finance and Book
   5 intertemporal reasoning.
2. Book 10 adds monetary and open-economy constraints while retrieving Book 6
   finance and Book 9 output-gap states.
3. Book 11 removes the single-book scaffold: students decompose prompts, select
   operations, apply correction-model discipline, and repair answers across
   mixed cases.

## Boundary Decisions

- Book 8 does not absorb macro stabilization.
- Book 9 does not absorb detailed monetary transmission.
- Book 10 does not become final-exam practice prematurely.
- Book 11 integrates Books 1-10 and does not add large new theory domains.

## Decision

The retrieval spine culminates in mixed CvTE-style cases, not isolated macro
chapter drills. It is ready for read-only lead review as planning architecture
only.
