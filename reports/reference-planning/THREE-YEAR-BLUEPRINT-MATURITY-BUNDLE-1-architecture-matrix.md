## Boundary Decisions

- Book 8 does not absorb macro stabilization; it keeps growth, public finance,
  strategy, and sustainability bridge content.
- Book 9 does not absorb detailed monetary transmission; it may name interest
  and central banks only as fiscal-policy context.
- Book 10 does not become final exam practice; it teaches monetary/open-economy
  macro.
- Book 11 does not introduce a large new theory domain; it trains exam
  operations and cumulative retrieval.

## Decision

The structural curriculum design is stable at planning level. Later changes to
book count, test-week placement, or major boundaries require a blueprint-change
review.
