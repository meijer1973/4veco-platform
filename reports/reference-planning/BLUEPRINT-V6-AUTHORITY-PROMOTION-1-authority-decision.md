- Three-year 11-book umbrella route.

- Book-level Year 2 and Year 3 planning envelopes.
- Book-level exam-operation spine planning.
- Sequencing of next strategic lanes:

## What v6 May Not Govern

- Detailed Year 1 paragraph baseline while v5 remains active.
- Target-exercise finality.
- MTU identity, dependencies, terms, or machine status.

- Year 2/3 paragraph target creation.
- Generated lesson output.
- Product-route adoption, CP-6, Scale Gate, diagnostics, mastery, PV,
  summative use, or student/product use.
