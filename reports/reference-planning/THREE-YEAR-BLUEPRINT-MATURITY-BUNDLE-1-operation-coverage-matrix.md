## Known Non-Unknown State

No operation row has an unknown evidence state:

- official evidence is either target-side safe, reviewed-family-only,
  partial/held, or missing but named;
- target status is reviewed, migrated/placeholder, missing, or non-target
  practice cycle;
- MTU/task-family status is live/partial, mirrored-skill-only, missing, held,
  or not applicable;
- production disposition is blocked, mapping-ready only, or deferred.

`OP-A3` and `OP-G3` are explicitly carried above because they are easy to lose
inside broader calculation/graph groups. `OP-A3` routes into Book 5/6
real-nominal/deflator target work. `OP-G3` routes into graph-state proof,
Q19-specific repair where applicable, and Year 3 IS-MB-GA graph/task-family
review.
