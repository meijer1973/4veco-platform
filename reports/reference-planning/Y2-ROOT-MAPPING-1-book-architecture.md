## Book 5

Book end-state: students can distinguish stocks from flows, reason across time
and generations, interpret pension/source tables, and write long-horizon
explanations without confusing Book 5 with Book 6 finance mechanics.

Proposed chapters:

| Chapter | Role | Paragraph candidates |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 Time And Economic Variables | Build stock/flow and time-horizon foundations. | `Y2-B5-P01` to `Y2-B5-P04` |
| 5.2 Intertemporal Exchange | Connect saving, borrowing, indexation, and lifecycle decisions. | `Y2-B5-P05` to `Y2-B5-P07` |
| 5.3 Pensions And Generations | Teach pension systems, ageing, and solidarity. | `Y2-B5-P08` to `Y2-B5-P11` |
| 5.4 Sustainability And Source Evaluation | Consolidate long-horizon policy reasoning and test-week retrieval. | `Y2-B5-P12` to `Y2-B5-P14` |

Formal test-week scope: stock/flow distinction, intertemporal exchange,
indexation/real-value reasoning, pension systems, ageing, sustainability, and
source-supported generation claims.

Cumulative retrieval from earlier books: Book 1 percentages/index numbers and
graph/table reading, Book 2 elasticity/welfare when policy trade-offs appear,
Book 3 intervention logic, and Book 4 labour/trade source reasoning.

Bridge to Book 6: interest is introduced only as the price of time and a
discounting signal; detailed interest-rate mechanics, bonds, housing, and
financial-market channels move to Book 6.

## Book 6

Book end-state: students can explain and calculate interest-rate effects,
interpret bond and housing-market mechanisms, read financial-market sources,
and prepare for later monetary-policy transmission without doing macro policy
inside Book 6.

Proposed chapters:

| Chapter | Role | Paragraph candidates |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 Interest Mechanics | Build nominal/real interest, compounding, and rate-change reasoning. | `Y2-B6-P01` to `Y2-B6-P04` |
| 6.2 Bonds And Financial Markets | Connect bond prices, risk/return/liquidity, and financial intermediation. | `Y2-B6-P05` to `Y2-B6-P07` |
| 6.3 Housing And Household Finance | Apply finance mechanics to mortgages, housing, and balance sheets. | `Y2-B6-P08` to `Y2-B6-P10` |
| 6.4 Financial Source Cases | Consolidate financial tables and prepare monetary/open-economy retrieval. | `Y2-B6-P11` to `Y2-B6-P14` |

Formal test-week scope: interest-rate mechanics, bond price/yield direction,
housing and household-finance cases, financial source tables, and mechanism
answers.

Cumulative retrieval from earlier books: Book 5 time/stock-flow, Book 1
percentages and indexation, Book 2 cost/revenue and elasticity when market
responses appear, and Book 4 labour/trade cases where financial conditions
affect choices.

Bridge to Book 7: financial risk and insurance language is introduced only as
application context; expected value, risk pooling, adverse selection, moral
hazard, and principal-agent reasoning move to Book 7.

## Book 7

Book end-state: students can calculate expected value or expected loss when
needed, explain insurance incentives, classify asymmetric-information problems,
and build source-supported risk answers. Q3 informs the insurance cost-threshold
architecture but does not close `OP-R1` or create registry records.

Proposed chapters:

| Chapter | Role | Paragraph candidates |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 Risk And Expected Value | Build risk, uncertainty, expected value/loss, and risk attitude. | `Y2-B7-P01` to `Y2-B7-P04` |
| 7.2 Insurance Design | Teach pooling, premiums, deductibles, and the Q3 insurance-threshold anchor. | `Y2-B7-P05` to `Y2-B7-P08` |
| 7.3 Information Problems | Teach moral hazard, adverse selection, principal-agent, screening, and signalling. | `Y2-B7-P09` to `Y2-B7-P12` |
| 7.4 Risk Source Cases | Consolidate cyber/health/contract risk and test-week retrieval. | `Y2-B7-P13` to `Y2-B7-P14` |

Formal test-week scope: expected-value/loss calculations, insurance contract
design, asymmetric information, moral hazard, adverse selection,
principal-agent problems, and source-supported incentive explanations.

Cumulative retrieval from earlier books: Book 1 percentage/table reading, Book
2 elasticity and surplus, Book 3 intervention incentives, Book 5 time and
Book 6 finance/housing contexts.

Bridge to Book 8: strategic behavior and collective-action problems are
introduced as risk/information consequences; payoff matrices, public goods,
redistribution, and growth bridge move to Book 8.

Q3 grouping decision: `q3-calc-1` maps to one compound planning anchor,
`Y2-Q3-COMPOUND-INSURANCE-THRESHOLD`, with child steps for annual
premium/deductible comparison, break-even threshold calculation, and threshold
conclusion with unit/direction. This prevents overlapping future operation
records unless a later schema explicitly models parent/child operations.

## Book 8

Book end-state: students can reason strategically, explain collective-action
and public-good failures, evaluate redistribution/public-finance trade-offs,
and connect growth/sustainability arguments to Book 9 macro without turning
Book 8 into a macro-policy book.

Proposed chapters:

| Chapter | Role | Paragraph candidates |
|---|---|---|
| 8.1 Strategic Interaction | Keep payoff logic, dominant strategy, Nash, and prisoner's-dilemma reasoning together. | `Y2-B8-P01` to `Y2-B8-P04` |
| 8.2 Collective Action And Public Goods | Connect free riding, public goods, welfare retrieval, and intervention design. | `Y2-B8-P05` to `Y2-B8-P08` |
| 8.3 Redistribution And Public Finance | Tie taxes, redistribution, budgets, debt, and incentives together. | `Y2-B8-P09` to `Y2-B8-P12` |
| 8.4 Growth And Sustainability Bridge | Prepare Book 9 macro through productivity, green GDP, circular economy, and policy evaluation. | `Y2-B8-P13` to `Y2-B8-P16` |

Formal test-week scope: payoff matrix reasoning, dominant strategy, prisoner's
dilemma, collective-action and public-good explanations, redistribution and
public-finance trade-offs, growth/sustainability source evaluation, and
constructed policy answers.

Cumulative retrieval from earlier books: Book 1 graph/table and answer-form
basics, Book 2 welfare and elasticity, Book 3 taxes/intervention, Book 4
market failure/trade/labour, Book 5 time/generation reasoning, Book 6 finance
mechanisms, and Book 7 risk/information.

Bridge to Book 9: Book 8 ends with growth, productivity, green GDP, and
public-finance trade-offs. Keynesian multipliers, output gaps, fiscal
stabilisation, macro shocks, and macro model diagrams are deferred to Book 9.

Q15 integration decision: Q15 supports the Book 8 strategic-interaction and
answer-construction architecture as a two-link planning anchor: first identify
dominant-strategy logic, then explain the mutually worse prisoner's-dilemma
outcome. It does not close `OP-S1` or `OP-ANS3`.
