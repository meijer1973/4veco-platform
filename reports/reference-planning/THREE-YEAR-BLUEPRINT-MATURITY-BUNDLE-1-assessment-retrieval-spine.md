## Retrieval Progression

| Stage | Retrieval function |
|---|---|
| Books 1-2 | establish calculation, graph/table, equilibrium, elasticity, welfare routines |
| Books 3-4 | retrieve those routines in policy, market-structure, labour, trade, and market-failure contexts |
| Books 5-6 | retrieve Year 1 measurement and source routines into time, interest, housing, and finance |
| Books 7-8 | retrieve calculation and incentive logic into risk, information, strategy, public goods, redistribution, and growth |
| Books 9-10 | retrieve public finance, finance, graph, labour/trade, and answer-form routines into macro policy and open-economy models |
| Book 11 | retrieve Books 1-10 under mixed official-style source pressure with correction-model and time discipline |

## Exam-Training Progression

1. Foundational operations: Books 1-4 introduce calculation, graph, source,
   and short-answer routines.
2. Context layering: Books 5-8 add time, finance, risk, information,
   strategic, public-finance, and growth contexts.
3. Macro policy integration: Books 9-10 add fiscal, monetary, model, and
   open-economy policy mechanisms.
4. Mixed CvTE-style operation training: Book 11 decomposes prompts, sources,
   correction models, answer forms, timed papers, repair, and retest.

## Decision

The assessment route and retrieval spine are fixed as planning baseline. Later
target packets must place their paragraph routes inside this spine rather than
redesigning the course structure.
