## Test-Week Shape

Book 5 test week should favor source-supported long-horizon explanations:
students classify stock/flow variables, use time-indexed data, explain pension
or sustainability mechanisms, and write a balanced intergenerational answer.

Book 6 test week should combine mechanisms and calculations: interest-rate
effects, bond price/yield direction, mortgage or housing-finance choices, and
financial table interpretation.

Book 7 test week should combine expected-value style reasoning with insurance
and information problems: premium/deductible logic, moral hazard, adverse
selection, principal-agent design, and source-supported risk conclusions.

Book 8 test week should use strategic and policy evaluation: payoff reasoning,
public-good/free-rider cases, redistribution and tax/debt trade-offs, and
growth/sustainability source arguments.

| Book 5 into Books 6-8 | Provides time, stock/flow, intergenerational, and sustainability logic for finance, insurance, and public-finance cases. |
| Book 6 into Books 7-8 | Provides finance, risk/return/liquidity, balance-sheet, and household-mechanism language. |
| Book 7 into Book 8 | Provides incentive, risk, information, contract, and expected-value language for strategic/public-good cases. |
| Book 8 into Books 9-11 | Provides policy evaluation, public-finance, growth/sustainability, and strategic-interaction foundations for macro and advanced policy work. |
