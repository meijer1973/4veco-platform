## Full-Paper Selection Protocol

Select papers by:

1. coverage of Books 1-10 operations;
2. source/table/graph variety;
3. correction-model point-allocation clarity;
4. answer-form variety;
5. presence of repairable errors across calculation, source reasoning, graph
   state, and constructed response;
6. exclusion of Q19-dependent closure until Q19 exits exact HOLD.

## Error-Log Schema

Each timed-paper attempt should produce:

- question locator;
- missed operation row;
- missed answer form;
- source/table/graph/correction-model gap;
- point-allocation loss;
- repair task;
- retest evidence;
- remaining blocker.

## Authority Boundary

Timed papers and repair cycles are training cycles, not target-registry rows.
They do not authorize grading, summative use, mastery, PV, diagnostics, or
student/product use.
