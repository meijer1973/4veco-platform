## Maturity Definition

v6 reaches planning end state when:

1. the 4+4+3 course route is fixed;
2. all eleven books have a stable role, assessment scope, retrieval function,
   and bridge;
3. the working load model is resolved;
4. every candidate has known status;
5. the exam-training progression is explicit;
6. every book and operation has known evidence state;
7. the production critical path is defined;
8. change control is defined.

This bundle marks those criteria as met at planning level.

## Change Control

After human approval, v6 becomes the frozen planning baseline. Later structural
changes require a `BLUEPRINT-CHANGE-REVIEW` packet if they alter:

- number of books;
- formal test-week mapping;
- book role or major boundary;
- count-bearing model;
- Book 11 module/cycle treatment;
- q22/q23 or Q19 boundary;
- production critical path ordering.

Small target-packet decisions do not require blueprint-change review when they
stay inside this baseline.

## Non-Authorization

Planning maturity is not product maturity. It does not authorize generated
lessons, product routes, CP-6, Scale Gate, diagnostics, mastery, PV, summative
use, or student/product use.
