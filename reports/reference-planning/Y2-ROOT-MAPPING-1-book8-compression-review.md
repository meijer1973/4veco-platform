## Compression Decision

Book 8 should use 16 paragraph candidates inside the v6 14-18 envelope.

The book is coherent if it is treated as a strategic and public-policy bridge:

1. Strategic interaction explains why individually rational choices can create
   collectively worse outcomes.
2. Public goods and collective action extend that logic into market failure and
   intervention design.
3. Redistribution and public finance give the policy trade-off frame.
4. Growth and sustainability prepare Book 9 without introducing macro-policy
   models early.

## Concepts That Belong Together

| Cluster | Include in Book 8 | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic interaction | payoff matrices, dominant strategy, Nash/prisoner's dilemma, self-binding | Q15 planning evidence and answer-construction practice belong before public-good cases. |
| Collective action | public goods, free riding, institution design, intervention cases | This is the natural policy application of strategic interaction. |
| Public finance | redistribution, tax incidence retrieval, budget balance, debt basics | These topics share equity/efficiency and incentive trade-offs. |
| Growth bridge | productivity, green GDP, circular economy, intergenerational sustainability | These prepare Book 9 while staying in long-run policy evaluation. |

## Concepts Deferred To Book 9

| Defer | Reason |
|---|---|
| Keynesian multiplier | Requires macro model sequence and belongs with Book 9 stabilization. |
| Output gaps | Needs aggregate-demand/aggregate-supply or equivalent macro framework. |
| Fiscal stabilisation | Should follow Book 8 public-finance basics but not be tested inside Book 8. |
| Macro shocks | Needs Book 9 macro transmission language. |
| Macro model diagrams | Not part of Book 8 strategic/public-finance bridge. |
| Monetary-policy transmission | Book 6 prepares finance mechanics; execution belongs later and Q19 remains HOLD. |

clusters and the Book 9 deferrals above. The compression decision is not a
production decision.
