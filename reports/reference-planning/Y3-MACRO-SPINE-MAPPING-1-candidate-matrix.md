candidates. Book 11 is not reduced to ordinary paragraphs: it has nine
count-bearing modules (`M01-M08` and `M13`), two timed-paper cycles (`M09` and
`M11`), two repair/retest cycles (`M10` and `M12`), and 13 total scheduled
modules/cycles.
