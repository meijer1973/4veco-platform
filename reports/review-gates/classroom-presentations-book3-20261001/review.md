## Serialized teaching narrative

The prior Book 2 presentations were inspected for actual teaching, rather than
assuming that a named prerequisite had been covered. Book 2 teaches surplus
triangles and model limits, price/output algebra, total versus average cost,
and interval marginal changes. It does not yet teach tax wedges, derivatives
or continuous profit optimization.

**3.1 government intervention.** Tax payment/receipt prices are introduced
before incidence and the public-revenue ledger. The subsidy lesson reverses the
wedge and subtracts expenditure on all sales, then explains loss from extra
trades. Price ceilings separate desired demand from actual constrained trade.
Price floors distinguish private sales from explicitly guaranteed public
purchases; the replacement production quota removes that purchase policy.
Mixed practice resets tax and ceiling alternatives explicitly. Each public
amount uses its own base: taxed sales, subsidized sales or actual purchases.

**3.2 the price-taking firm.** Market Q and firm q are separated, including the
thousands scale, before the horizontal P=GO=MO line. The derivative is taught as
point marginal cost and contrasted with the previously taught interval average
and exact next-unit change. Profit maximization then requires marginal direction,
capacity and the zero-output boundary. The profit rectangle uses per-unit axes,
explicitly distinguished from Book 2's total-euro profit difference. The mixed
lesson combines these operations in two short-run market states with unchanged
firm count; it does not assume Book 4 entry/exit into the sequence.

**3.3 international trade.** Opportunity cost is refreshed before qualitative
comparative advantage, specialization, attractive exchange terms and effects on
different groups. The small-country open-market model then separates domestic
production, domestic consumption and imports/exports. That import availability
is an explicit model change from the earlier closed-market shortage. Tariffs
tax only remaining imports and do not shift domestic supply; free import quotas
do not automatically generate government tariff receipts. Mixed practice applies
those distinctions to a complete source-based target without inventing numerical
comparative-advantage ratios or untaught quota-rent allocation.

New-content starts remain supported exploration, with visible reading support
and a return after instruction before independent work. Chapter-wide numbering,
guided 22A/40A and extra 47A retain their actual source roles. All full target
contexts, figures/tables and questions precede their answers; own teaching
examples use separate contexts/data rather than consuming the assigned practice.

across the reviewed sequence. The observed edition, placement, graph-label and
conversion problems belong to the presentation workflow. No textbook change was

tax-oriented welfare-loss definition should include the extra-trade subsidy
case already correctly taught in 3.1.3, and literal Markdown heading markers

paragraph and name known pacing conflicts. File review does not demonstrate a
single-period fit, measured pupil mastery or formal approval of target candidates.
