## Authority and validation implications

The active Book 3/4 outline metadata's `current_sha256` and hyperlink-only `technical_projection` claim would become stale after replacing route prose. Keep `original_sha256`, received-delivery references, historical selection provenance, structural rows, and approval boundaries intact. Record the bounded current amendment separately.

This cannot be achieved by merely refreshing those current hashes: `build-scripts/references/books34-selected-structure.js` calls `migrate-books34-v3.parseOutline`, which accepts only the exact received bytes or exact hyperlink-only projection. The historical migration explicitly says not to repin history for later editions. Current selection validation therefore needs an explicit, finite amendment identity/path that continues to verify unchanged structural rows and refuses unrelated changes. Do not rerun or relax the historical migration as if the amended outline were the original delivery.

Similarly `books34-v3-delivery.js` validates the immutable manifest identity and exactly 813 delivered files plus the manifest. Live current edits inside the lesson package cannot pass that receipt check unchanged. Distinguish the current derivative/revision from the immutable receipt; preserve the received manifest and transport provenance rather than rewriting them to describe new bytes.

Book 2's outline metadata includes the exercise-builder in `authority_sources`. Changes to that skill require a currentness/evidence refresh through the applicable bounded authority workflow. Do not imply that the old hash or an old approval covers the new semantics.
