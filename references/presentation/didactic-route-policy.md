# Didactic Route Policy

Status: reusable economics presentation route policy.

Derived from `references/exemplars/1.1.1-golden-presentation/`.

## Core Rule

A production economics presentation must expose a coherent learning route, but
the exact eleven-slide route from the §1.1.1 exemplar is mandatory only for that
exemplar. Other presentations should compose the universal roles below with the
conditional roles that the concept actually needs.

## Universal Roles

Every production economics presentation must include these instructional jobs,
though one slide may satisfy more than one job when the route remains clear:

- `route_contract`: goal, route, and success criteria.
- `concept_model_development`: canonical concept or model development.
- `worked_example_or_application`: at least one application, worked example, or source-linked use of the concept.
- `retrieval_check`: a short check of immediate understanding where it strengthens the route.
- `summary_bridge`: summary and bridge to the next learning surface.
- `student_facing_notes`: student-readable explanations beyond slide headlines.

## Conditional Roles

Use these roles when the topic needs them. Do not add them as quota slides.

- `narrative_anchor`: a concrete case, person, source, or situation before abstraction.
- `transfer_slide`: the same pattern across another context or actor.
- `misconception_slide`: common error, contrast, or repair when the topic invites a predictable misconception.
- `procedure_route`: named steps when students need a repeatable method.
- `calculation_interpretation_split`: separate setup/calculation from economic meaning when a numeric operation and interpretation are both central.

## §1.1.1 Exemplar Route

The accepted §1.1.1 Golden Presentation Exemplar uses this exact sequence:

1. `route_contract`
2. `narrative_anchor`
3. `concept_model_development`
4. `transfer_slide`
5. `concept_model_development`
6. `misconception_slide`
7. `procedure_route`
8. `worked_example_calculation`
9. `worked_example_interpretation`
10. `retrieval_check`
11. `summary_bridge`

This sequence is not a global presentation quota. It is the fixed route for the
accepted §1.1.1 source snapshot.

## Retrieval Check Boundary

A retrieval/check slide may check immediate understanding and support memory
retrieval. It does not replace the paragraph's exercise or check surfaces.

A retrieval/check slide may not claim diagnostics, mastery, summative status,
automatic sequencing, target-equivalent proof, or completion authority.

## Worked Example Boundary

Calculation/setup and interpretation/conclusion should be separated when the
concept has both a numeric step and an economic meaning. Students should see:

- what is chosen;
- what is not chosen;
- what the best non-chosen alternative is;
- how the calculation works;
- what the result means.
