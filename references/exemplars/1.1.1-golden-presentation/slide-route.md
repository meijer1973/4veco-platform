# Slide Route

Status: exact conceptual golden route for the accepted §1.1.1 web-first presentation.

This sequence is mandatory for `1.1.1-golden-presentation` only. Reusable
presentation policy separates universal route duties from conditional route
tools.

## Required Sequence

The successful route is:

1. Route contract.
2. Concrete narrative anchor.
3. Core concept.
4. Transfer across levels.
5. Second concept.
6. Misconception control.
7. Canonical procedure.
8. Worked example step 1-2.
9. Worked example step 3-4.
10. Active check.
11. Summary and bridge.

## Role Contract

| Slide role | Learning move | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `route_contract` | Tell students what they will learn, why it matters, and how the route works. | Goal, route, and success criteria are visible. |
| `narrative_anchor` | Start from a concrete recognizable case. | Named character or context, numbers visible before abstraction. |
| `concept_model_development` | Form the canonical concept or model. | Canonical term, definition, and relation to the anchor. |
| `transfer_slide` | Show that the same pattern works elsewhere. | Additional contexts where they clarify the concept. |
| `misconception_slide` | Prevent common wrong answers before practice. | Visible "niet verwarren met ..." or equivalent. |
| `procedure_route` | Make the reusable procedure visible. | Named steps and student action per step. |
| `worked_example_calculation` | Compute before concluding. | Calculation/setup separated from interpretation. |
| `worked_example_interpretation` | Explain chosen, not chosen, and best non-chosen alternative. | Explicit alternative-cost and nettowaarde statement. |
| `retrieval_check` | Let students apply the concept lightly. | Formative prompt, not a full exercise set. |
| `summary_bridge` | Restate the mental model and point forward. | Key takeaways plus next learning surface. |

## 1.1.1 Instantiation

For `1.1.1 Schaarste en economisch denken`, the route uses Lisa with €20 as the anchor, then transfers schaarste to producers and government, introduces alternatieve kosten, controls the price/zeldzaamheid/sum pitfalls, uses the B02 four-step procedure, and ends with a brief Lisa/farmer retrieval check before summarizing the five core sentences.

## Authority Boundary

This route is a design standard. It does not authorize broad migration, lesson-output editing, Scale Gate claims, diagnostics, mastery, automatic sequencing, summative use, or target-equivalent completion language.
