# Reasoning Game Golden Family

Status: versioned golden-exemplar library for `GOAL-REASONING-GOLDEN-FAMILY-1`.

This library preserves four human-calibrated reasoning-game exemplars and turns their shared product standard into repository capability. The prototypes are evidence, not a deployable student product.

## Rule

```text
copy product grammar
re-derive reasoning grammar
```

Use the exemplars to copy durable product grammar: coherent source-to-task loop, prominent non-answer-giving goal, local repair, once-per-session stable shuffle, meaningful distractors, visible answer functions, feedback after attempt, and rendered interaction-state proof.

Do not copy a mechanic just because it exists in a prototype. For a new paragraph, re-derive the reasoning grammar from the paragraph target, source material, and likely misconception.

## Canonical Files

- `manifest.json` is the versioned source of truth for exemplar IDs, hashes, authority boundaries, and review state.
- `family-standard.md` is the cross-exemplar product and transfer standard.
- `transfer-matrix.md` records which student actions should become shared task-shell capability.
- `reasoning-archetype-decision-tree.md` routes new paragraphs to a reasoning archetype before mechanics are chosen.
- `change-notes.md` records stable policy changes and adoption boundaries.
- `policy-traceability.json` maps exemplar decisions to policy, skill instructions, validators, fixtures, and review questions.

## Authority Boundary

The exemplars authorize golden-reference learning only. They do not authorize student rollout, diagnostics, mastery decisions, automatic sequencing, summative use, product-value projection, target-equivalent proof, or Scale Gate claims.

`GATE-REASONING-GOLDEN-FAMILY-1` is the only human gate for this adoption package.
