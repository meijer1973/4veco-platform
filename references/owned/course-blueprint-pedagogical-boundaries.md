# Course Blueprint Pedagogical Boundaries

Status: owned curriculum-design clarification
Applies to: `course-blueprint-v5.md` and the Year 1 detail embedded by `course-blueprint-v6-three-year.md`
Human decision date: 2026-08-30
Change type: interpretive clarification; no change to book counts, book roles, assessment calendar, or target-exercise registry
Book 1 second-edition backlog: https://github.com/meijer1973/4veco-platform/issues/221

## Purpose

The course blueprint defines the intended sequence of terminal learning targets and count-bearing curriculum units. It does **not** require textbook explanations to contain only terminal learning goals.

A strong textbook may deliberately introduce a representation, term, relation, or supplied formula before its later formal treatment when that earlier encounter helps students understand the active concept. This is **anticipatory scaffolding**, not automatic scope drift.

The exercise-first hierarchy remains intact: reviewed target exercises determine what students must be able to do independently at the paragraph target level. Pedagogical scaffolding may support that route but does not silently become a target, prerequisite, or mastery claim.

## Three distinct statuses

### 1. Terminal target

A concept, procedure, representation, or answer form is terminal for the paragraph when the reviewed target exercise or stronger authority requires students to perform it independently at the intended cognitive level.

Terminal targets may be assessed without the answer path being supplied.

### 2. Anticipatory scaffold or preview

A concept, representation, term, or formula may appear before its formal terminal placement when it improves understanding of the active target.

A preview may include:

- a short definition;
- an intuitive example;
- a simple numerical application;
- an illustrative graph or representation;
- a supplied formula used for substitution or interpretation;
- terminology that will be formalised later.

A preview is not evidence that students have mastered the later topic.

### 3. Prerequisite mastery

A later paragraph may assume earlier knowledge without reteaching only when the earlier material is itself a reviewed target/prerequisite or another explicit planning decision authorises that assumption.

Mere exposure is not prerequisite mastery.

## Quality rule: no pedagogical concessions

Blueprint alignment must not be enforced by stripping away useful explanations simply because the explanation goes beyond the literal wording of a terminal target.

Keep anticipatory material when it materially improves conceptual understanding and remains proportionate. Remove or compress it when it creates unnecessary cognitive load, displaces the active target, becomes a hidden second curriculum, or is later treated as mastered without sufficient evidence.

The standard is therefore **pedagogically justified depth**, not target-only minimalism.

## Allowed anticipatory patterns

### Consumer surplus before the formal surplus block

Book 1 may use a bounded introduction to consumer surplus while teaching willingness to pay and individual demand. A discrete example such as `betalingsbereidheid - marktprijs`, including a simple visual or total over a few purchased units, can make the demand concept more meaningful.

This does not move the formal surplus/welfare block out of Book 2. Book 2 §2.3.1 must still teach consumer surplus as formal content at the depth required by its reviewed target and subsequent welfare analysis. Book 1 exposure counts as familiarity only.

### Step functions as a didactic bridge

A step-function demand representation may be used to connect discrete willingness-to-pay decisions to a continuous or linear demand model. The representation is a teaching instrument unless a reviewed target explicitly makes step-function construction or analysis a required operation.

Students may therefore see and use a step function without the blueprint claiming that step functions are a terminal exam goal.

### Supplied later-book formulas

An earlier exercise may supply a formula whose formal conceptual treatment belongs to a later book when the actual student operation is appropriately limited.

For example, if `winst = omzet - kosten` is explicitly supplied, a Book 1 task may ask students to read the relevant data, substitute values, calculate, or use the result inside an opportunity-cost comparison. That is not equivalent to requiring students to recall, derive, select, or conceptually master the profit relation independently.

The later Book 2 treatment must still teach costs, revenue, profit, break-even, and related procedures as formal content.

### Light terminology before formal classification

Terms such as `normaal goed` and `inferieur goed` may appear in Book 1 to explain the direction of an income-driven demand shift. Unless the active target requires classification, Book 1 exposure must not be treated as mastery of the income-elasticity classification system.

Book 2 §2.2.3 remains responsible for formal classification through `Ei` and for the later normal/inferior and necessity/luxury distinctions required by its target.

## Limits on anticipatory scaffolding

Anticipatory material is acceptable only when all of the following hold:

1. It strengthens understanding of the active paragraph target.
2. It is bounded enough that the paragraph does not become a hidden treatment of a later unit.
3. It does not replace practice of an operation that the active target actually requires.
4. Students are not later assumed to have mastered it merely because they encountered it.
5. The later formal unit still teaches the concept or operation at the required depth.
6. Consolidation paragraphs remain consolidation-first; previews inside them must be especially constrained because `gemengde_opgaven` introduce no new terminal theory.
7. Reviews distinguish `seen`, `supported`, and `independently required` operations instead of treating every noun or formula in student material as a target.

## Compatibility with the Book 2+ Part A authoring contract

For newly authored Book 2 and later theory paragraphs,
`skills/econ-exercise-builder.md` remains the operational authority for
backward design, the seven-section sequence, target-operation coverage, the
paper route, scaffolding/fading, and the whole-lesson time equation. This owned
clarification interprets the status of earlier exposure; it does not relax or
replace that Part A contract.

Inside a Part A paragraph, anticipatory material may support only:

- explanation or context for an approved lesson goal or target operation;
- retrieval of a prerequisite that was already taught and may therefore be
  retrieved under the existing Startopgaven rule; or
- an explicitly optional perspective that does not become part of the core
  route or its assessment claim.

A preview by itself may not fill a `Covered` cell in the required
lesson-goal/target-operation alignment table. It is not evidence of mastery,
and it may not introduce a new independently required operation in the worked
example, the current-content Startopgaven check, Begeleide inoefening,
Zelfstandige oefening, or Doeloefening unless that operation is authorised by
the reviewed target exercise or an approved lesson goal.

Preview material must not displace practice of an approved target operation or
defeat the paragraph's explicit whole-lesson equation of at most 55 minutes.
If a useful preview does not fit after the full target route is protected,
compress or defer the preview; do not remove a target operation to make room.

The same boundary applies when later teaching follows an earlier supplied
formula. Book 1 familiarity with a supplied cost or profit relation must not
shorten the formal Book 2 teaching. In particular, Book 2 §2.1.1 must still
teach its reviewed cost relations and procedures in full, and later Book 2
revenue, profit, and break-even teaching must still provide its own formal
instruction and target-level practice.

## Edition policy for Book 1

The first edition of Book 1 is already printed and is frozen for the current school year. Blueprint clarification on 2026-08-30 does not authorise retroactive edits to that printed edition.

Findings fall into two categories:

- **clarification, not defect**: useful anticipatory material that can remain when its status is understood correctly;
- **second-edition backlog**: genuine errors, target-operation omissions, unnecessary over-formalisation, stale references, or repository hygiene problems to address when preparing Book 1 second edition.

The durable second-edition backlog is tracked in issue #221. Do not block Book 2 first-edition production on Book 1 print changes that cannot be made this year.

## Consequences for Book 2 first edition

Book 2 authors must use Book 1 preview material as a continuity advantage, not as a licence to skip teaching.

- **Costs/revenue/profit:** Book 1 supplied-formula encounters are familiarity only. Book 2 §2.1.1 teaches its reviewed cost relations and procedures in full; later Chapter 2.1 revenue, profit, and break-even teaching is likewise not shortened by that exposure.
- **Normal/inferior goods:** Book 1 terminology is familiarity only. Book 2 §2.2.3 formalises classification through income elasticity.
- **Consumer surplus:** Book 1 may have introduced the intuition. Book 2 §2.3.1 must reactivate and then deepen the concept, including the graphical and target-level operations needed for the later surplus/welfare sequence.
- **Step functions:** do not assume step-function construction as prerequisite mastery unless a Book 2 reviewed target explicitly requires it.

This policy allows deliberate spiral exposure while preserving a clear distinction between first encounter, formal instruction, independent target performance, and later cumulative use.

## Authority boundary

This clarification does not:

- change the 12/12/14/16 Year 1 book counts;
- move any formal terminal paragraph from one book to another;
- mutate `references/authored/course-target-exercises.json`;
- override reviewed target exercises;
- authorise target claims from incidental textbook wording;
- reopen the printed first edition of Book 1;
- authorise companion/web work;
- change the v6 11-book / 4+4+3 course route.

Where an actual lesson and a reviewed target disagree about an independently required operation, the disagreement remains a real alignment issue even if the lesson also contains legitimate anticipatory scaffolding.
