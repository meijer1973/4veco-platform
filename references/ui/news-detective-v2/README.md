NEWS-DETECTIVE-V2-PROTOTYPE-REPLACEMENT-1
=========================================

`approved.html` is the canonical visual and structural baseline for
NEWS-DETECTIVE-V2-PROTOTYPE-REPLACEMENT-1.

Production Nieuws-detective pages must be built from this V2 structure.

Do not rebuild from:
- the old `newsdetective.css` layout;
- the old `newsdetective-ui.js` `buildHTML` structure;
- the old `nd-header` / `nd-container` shell;
- the compact two-line article card.

The existing engine and data may be reused as backend behavior only.
